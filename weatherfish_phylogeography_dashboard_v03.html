Data & methods

ROH totals are sums of PLINK calls ≥100 kb, not FROH; the displayed classes are 0.1–1 Mb, 1–5 Mb and ≥5 Mb. IBS values are raw PLINK distance units, not normalized mismatch fractions. Y1/Y2/Y3 are Y-associated groups, not validated haploid Y haplotypes. M1-xx/M2-xx are exact mitochondrial sequence haplotype IDs, not phylogenetic ranks.

Meet the species

European weatherfish

Misgurnus fossilis · grote modderkruiper

A secretive, mainly nocturnal fish of shallow, plant-rich wetlands and muddy ditches. Dark longitudinal stripes and ten barbels are distinctive. Skin and gut respiration help it survive oxygen-poor water and temporary drying.
