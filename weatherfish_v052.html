Data & methods

fROH is the summed PLINK ROH length divided by 1,004,448,232 bp across 86 selected diploid-like contigs (≥100 kb, ≥1 Mb, ≥5 Mb), not a conventional fraction of the full tetraploid genome. Genetic distances use PLINK 1−IBS (unitless, 0–1; panel dependent). Nuclear RD-like versus NL ghost-lineage affinity is conditional on Klooster-ascertained 100-kb windows, not a verified genomic ancestry proportion. Y1/Y2/Y3 are exploratory Y-associated classes. M1/M2 labels indicate mitochondrial NL/RD lineage only.

Meet the species

European weatherfish

Misgurnus fossilis · grote modderkruiper

A secretive, mainly nocturnal fish of shallow, plant-rich wetlands and muddy ditches. Dark longitudinal stripes and ten barbels are distinctive. Skin and gut respiration help it survive oxygen-poor water and temporary drying.
