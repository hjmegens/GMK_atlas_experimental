Interpretation & provenance

What the tracks do—and do not—show

ROH totals are from PLINK calls ≥100 kb, not FROH; class bars represent 0.1–1 Mb, 1–5 Mb and ≥5 Mb. IBS values are raw supplied PLINK distance units, not normalized mismatch fractions. Y1/Y2/Y3 are Y-associated groups, not validated haploid Y haplotypes. M1-xx/M2-xx are exact alignment-sequence IDs assigned for this prototype, not phylogenetic ranks.
